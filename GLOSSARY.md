# Takam Directives

The domain of the Takam (תכ"ם) site: the Accountant General's binding directives for government ministries, and how employees find and read them.

## Language

**Directive** (הוראה):
A document an employee reads to know what to do: an Instruction (הוראה), a Temporary Instruction (הוראת שעה) or a Message (הודעה).
_Avoid_: Document (for these), instruction (for all three), regulation

**Document** (מסמך):
Any record on the site, of any of its types, including forms, tenders and attachments, which are not Directives.
_Avoid_: File, record

**Directive type** (סוג מידע):
Which of the three kinds a Directive is; shown as a label next to its title.
_Avoid_: Category, kind

**Chapter** (פרק):
A node in the site's numbered hierarchy (e.g. 13 › 13.10) that groups directives by subject.
_Avoid_: Category, section

**Document code** (קוד מסמך):
The dotted identifier of a document, e.g. `H.13.10.1` or `HZ.13.10.1.1`; the prefix encodes the document type and the numbers encode the chapter path.
_Avoid_: ID, namecode

**Edition** (מהדורה):
One dated version of a document; a document has many editions over time and only the latest is shown.
_Avoid_: Revision

**In force** (בתוקף):
A document is in force today when its valid-from date has passed and its valid-to date is empty or has not yet passed (inclusive).
_Avoid_: Valid, active, isvalid

**Expired** (לא בתוקף):
A document whose valid-to date has passed.
_Avoid_: Invalid, archived

**Citation** (הפניה):
A numbered reference `[n]` in a chatbot answer that points to a Directive the answer was drawn from.
_Avoid_: Source, footnote, reference
